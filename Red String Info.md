RED STRING

Description:

Red String is an advocacy and awareness website dedicated to fighting corruption, promoting transparency, and encouraging government accountability in the Philippines. It aims to educate and empower citizens by providing accessible information about corruption, misuse of public funds, unfinished government projects, and how these issues directly affect communities, especially those living in poverty. Through its educational content, the website provides information about important laws such as Republic Act No. 3019, also known as the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act, helping users understand their rights and the laws that protect the public against corrupt practices. It also features facts and awareness articles that explain the causes and effects of corruption, as well as success stories of completed government projects and community initiatives that demonstrate the positive impact of transparency and responsible public service.

Red String also provides a platform where users can express their concerns and raise awareness about questionable activities through its contact form and anonymous reporting feature. Users can submit reports, share relevant information, and upload images or supporting evidence to help document their concerns. With its user-friendly navigation and accessible resources, the website encourages citizens to become more informed, observant, and involved in monitoring public projects and government activities. Ultimately, Red String aims to turn public awareness into meaningful action by connecting citizens with knowledge and opportunities to promote accountability. It believes that fighting corruption begins with an informed and responsible community, and through collective awareness and participation, it hopes to contribute to a society where public resources are properly managed, government officials are held accountable, and every Filipino has a fair opportunity to benefit from public services and development.

Project Objectives:
1. Promote Legal Literacy and Civic Awareness: Present plain-language explanations of Republic Act No. 3019 (the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act) alongside curated Facts and Awareness content to help citizens understand how corruption fuels poverty and inequality.
2. Showcase Transparency Benchmarks: Highlight verified, completed government and community initiatives through a Success Stories section to demonstrate that accountable governance and proper fund utilization are achievable.
3. Provide Safe and Accessible Reporting Channels: Implement a validated Contact Form featuring Anonymous Reporting, Image Upload and Preview, and automated Email Submission (via Formspree) so users can securely submit concerns and visual proof of stalled or substandard projects.
4. Ensure Seamless User Experience: Build an intuitive, responsive website structure complete with a clear Navigation Bar, Home, About, and Meet Our Team sections so visitors of varying technical backgrounds can easily access information and take informed civic action.
5. Support Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): Align digital advocacy with SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), and SDG 1 (No Poverty) by encouraging public accountability and fair distribution of government resources.


Features:

Navigation Bar – allows users to easily navigate between different sections of the website.

Home Section – introduces the Red String website and its purpose of promoting transparency and fighting corruption.

About Section – provides information about the website, its mission, and its vision.

RA No. 3019 – gives users information about their rights and the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act.

Facts and Awareness – presents important information about corruption and its effects on society.

Success Stories – displays images and information about government projects and community activities that were successfully completed.

Contact Form – allows users to send messages, concerns, or reports through the website.

Anonymous Reporting – gives users the option to submit their concerns without providing their name.

Image Upload and Preview – allows users to attach proof or images and preview their selected files before submitting.

Form Validation – checks if the required information is complete before allowing the form submission.

Email Submission – uses Formspree to process and send submitted reports to the designated email address.

Meet Our Team – introduces the people behind the Red String website
