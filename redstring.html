LAW SUMMARY

Republic Act No. 3019 Anti Graft and Corrupt Practices Act

Overview

Republic Act No. 3019, commonly known as the Anti Graft and Corrupt Practices Act, serves as the cornerstone of the Philippines' legal framework against corruption. This law is designed to ensure that public officials carry out their duties with integrity, transparency, and accountability, promoting trust between government institutions and the citizens they serve.

Why It Matters

Corruption undermines democracy, slows economic growth, and erodes public confidence in institutions. RA 3019 empowers citizens and the government alike to identify, prevent, and punish corrupt practices, making it a critical tool for promoting good governance and ethical leadership.

Key Provisions Include

  • Prohibition of Bribery and Gifts: Public officials are strictly forbidden from receiving any gifts, favors, or benefits that may influence their official duties.
  • Accountability in Decision-Making: Officials must perform their duties impartially, avoiding favoritism or discrimination in contracts, appointments, or allocation of public resources.
  • Protection of Public Funds: Misuse, embezzlement, or mismanagement of government funds and properties is punishable by law.
  • Legal Consequences: Violators of RA 3019 face administrative penalties, civil liabilities, and criminal prosecution, ensuring that corrupt practices are met with appropriate consequences.

Facts & awareness

Read these numbers twice.

Real figures from the 2025 flood control scandal and the latest corruption ranking.

70%

of flood control money may have been stolen

The Finance Secretary told the Senate in 2025 that up to 70% of funds for flood control projects were lost to corruption.

Source: Senate hearing, Sept 2025, as reported by The Star and SCMP

₱118.5B

lost in a single year

Officials estimated that ₱42.3 billion to ₱118.5 billion was lost every year from 2023 to 2025. That is up to US$2 billion in just two years.

Source: Finance Secretary Ralph Recto, Senate budget hearing

₱545B

program, plagued by "ghost projects"

The flood control program exposed in 2025 was worth more than ₱545 billion. Investigators looked into projects that were paid for but allegedly never built.

Source: 2025 State of the Nation Address; Egypt Independent / AP

120th

out of 182 countries

In the 2025 Corruption Perceptions Index the Philippines scored 32 out of 100. That is its worst result since the current scoring began in 2012.

Source: Transparency International, CPI 2025

2 of 11

Southeast Asian countries did worse

Only Cambodia and Myanmar ranked lower than the Philippines in the region. The global average score was 42, and ours was 32.

Source: Transparency International, CPI 2025; GMA News

0

new flood control projects in 2026

After the scandal, the government froze flood control spending. Meanwhile, Filipinos still face waist-high floods every typhoon season.

Source: The Star, Dec 2025; Gulf News
